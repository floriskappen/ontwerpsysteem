# States

States follow a lifecycle metaphor:
1. **Fallow (Empty)**: Renders tilled but empty ground rules (parallel stripes).
2. **Germinating (Loading)**: Renders a growing spiral seed head filling in golden angle order.
3. **Ripe (Complete)**: Swells and deepens color (no green ticks).
4. **Rising (Progress)**: A vessel filling with water from the bottom edge in stepped ticks.

## The Static Status Mark

A status mark is the **inert rest frame of a lifecycle state**, not a second status
language: pass is *ripe at its resting pose* (the accent), fail keeps the destructive
pigment confined to a thin rule, warn stays quiet. It is the compliant static fallback
for surfaces that cannot mount the animated lifecycle states above — one status metaphor,
frozen where it comes to rest. Marks show status with dots and rules only; never a green
tick, never a checkbox glyph, and fail never floods its box.
